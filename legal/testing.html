SoloPOS records sales, inventory and tables or workstations on your Android phone or tablet. It is an administrative tool; it does not process bank payments.

Codes are limited and subject to availability. Wait for confirmation before installing. No purchase or positive review is requested.

Can you join us for 14 days? Stay opted in for 14 consecutive days, use the app on several occasions and share honest feedback. Use fictional products and sales only.

Four steps to install

Use the same Google account in the group, the testing page and Google Play. These setup steps need internet; you can then test local features offline.

  1. Join the Google Group

    Open “SoloPOS - Prueba cerrada” and choose to join. This grants access to the participant group.

    Open the Google Group →

  2. Opt into the test

    Open the testing page and choose “Become a tester”. Joining the group alone does not enroll you in the Play test.

    Become a tester →

  3. Redeem your code

    Enter the code assigned to you. Each code is single-use and grants the paid app without a purchase. Redemption does not replace opting into the test.

    Redeem in Google Play →

  4. Install SoloPOS

    Return to the test page and follow its Google Play download link. Check that the same Google account is selected.

    Return to the test and install →

Your first test

  • Create a fictional product with 10 units, sell 2, then close and reopen the app. Check that 8 remain.
  • Add an area and a table or workstation. Try changing its shape, size and state.
  • Turn on airplane mode and try local sales and inventory features.
  • Send your phone model, Android version, what you tried and what happened. Screenshots should contain fictional data only.

Need help?

Google Play shows a price or an access error

Please do not pay to help with this invitation. Check that you joined the group, opted into the test and selected the same account throughout. If your code fails or a price remains, email the exact error so we can help before you buy anything.

Is this free? Is there a subscription?

Volunteers assigned a valid code can obtain the app without paying. SoloPOS is intended as a one-time purchase with no subscription. Requesting a code does not guarantee an allocation.

Do I need a printer or real business data?

No. You can test sales, inventory and tables without a printer. Do not use real customer or business data. If you already have SoloPOS with important data, contact us before replacing or uninstalling it or restoring a backup.

Can I share this invitation?

Yes, share this page. Each person should request their own code and complete the steps with their own account. Do not share a code assigned to someone else.
